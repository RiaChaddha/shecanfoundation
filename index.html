MENSTRUAL HEALTH AWARENESS

Millions of girls still miss school because of period poverty.

We work to ensure that menstrual hygiene never becomes a barrier to education, confidence, dignity, or opportunity.

Empower

Education, leadership, and independence for every woman.

Support

Dignity kits, menstrual health, and grassroots care.

OUR STORY

Born from a simple belief — no woman should be limited by circumstance.

She Can Foundation was founded by a group of individuals who shared a vision of creating a world where every woman has the opportunity to thrive and succeed.

Advocate

Breaking stigma and raising awareness at every level.

Collaborate

Working with communities to create lasting change.

OUR PROGRAMS

Creating an Impact

We believe empowerment starts with access, awareness, and opportunity. Our programs focus on menstrual health, education, community outreach, and women’s wellbeing, helping thousands of girls and women across India receive the support and resources they deserve.

Dignity Kits

Providing sanitary pads and essential hygiene resources to underserved communities.

Awareness Workshops

Breaking stigma through menstrual health education and open community discussions.

Community Support

Working with schools and local groups to create sustainable impact for women.
